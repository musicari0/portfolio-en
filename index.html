Services

The back of the record: what I do and how long each piece runs.

Side AServices

  1. Sonic identity design

    2–3 wks

    I create the brand’s musical DNA from scratch: an audit of the space, sonic profiles by zone and time of day, a base curation and a certification plaque. One-time payment.

  2. Ongoing curation

    Monthly

    Updates every week, rotation based on performance, control of listening fatigue and adjustments based on what you see on the floor.

  3. Music consulting

    1 wk

    For brands that already have music and want an outside ear. I audit what plays today, identify what works and deliver a clear action plan.

  4. Sound design for spaces

    Per project

    Retail, hospitality, restaurants and experience spaces: curation, integration with your audio system and atmosphere by time of day.

Side BHow I work

  1. Call

    20 min

    You tell me about the space, the audience and how you want it to feel. The first conversation is free.

  2. Space audit

    On site

    I walk the place, its zones and its hours, and listen to what plays there today.

  3. Identity delivery

    3 wks

    You receive the sonic identity ready to play in your space.
