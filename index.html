Sectors

Four kinds of space, four different problems. Sound solves part of each one.

Sector 01Hospitality

Hotels

Guests remember the first impression

A hotel has many spaces and one name. We design how each one sounds so the whole stay feels like the same house.

  • LobbyA warm welcome in the morning, more presence at night.
  • Restaurant and barFrom a quiet breakfast to the evening cocktail.
  • Spa and poolSlow tempo, no vocals, to slow things down.
  • Halls and elevatorsA short, constant sound signature.

GoalMore spending at the bar and better reviews.

Sector 02Retail and leisure

Shoppingmalls

Visitors decide how long they stay

Common areas sell too. A sound that goes with the stroll makes people walk more, visit more stores and come back.

  • Common areasTempo that rises at peak hours and drops in quiet ones.
  • Food courtMore energy without raising the volume.
  • SeasonsChristmas, back to school, anniversary: each campaign with its own sound.
  • EventsDJ sets and activations with the same identity.

GoalLonger visits and more stores visited.

Sector 03Health and wellness

Clinics

Patients arrive tense

In a clinic, waiting weighs on people. Music chosen so time passes more lightly, without intruding or competing with the staff’s voices.

  • ReceptionA calm tone that builds trust from the entrance.
  • Waiting roomLow tempo, no lyrics, a volume that lets people talk.
  • Exam roomsSilence or a very soft background, depending on the specialty.
  • Staff areasMusic for long shifts.

GoalA calmer wait and a brand that feels caring.

Sector 04Food and drink

Restaurants

Diners decide on another round

Music is on the menu too. Food brings them in. The atmosphere makes them stay, order more and come back.

  • LunchA lively pace that turns tables without rushing.
  • DinnerA slow tempo that invites dessert and coffee.
  • BarEnergy builds and the next round orders itself.
  • Waiting for a tableThe wait turns into the first drink.

GoalHigher checks and guests who book again.

Sensory proposal

The back cover: what’s included and how we start.

Side AThe experience

  1. Sound map of the space

    2–3 wks

    We walk the place, split it by zones and hours, and define how each one should feel: energy, tempo, volume and whether it has vocals.

  2. Sonic identity

    One-time fee

    The brand’s musical DNA, written into a sonic brand book any team can follow.

  3. A sound signature of your own

    Production

    A short piece produced for the brand, played at the entrance, in the elevators, in announcements and on social media.

  4. Ongoing curation

    Monthly

    Weekly rotation so staff don’t tire of the music, seasonal adjustments and reports on what works.

  5. Audio and operation

    On site

    We check the sound system and leave it scheduled by zone and by hour. No one on the team has to choose what plays.

Side BHow we start

  1. Call

    20 min

    You tell us about the space, the audience and how you want it to feel. The first conversation is free.

  2. Walkthrough and diagnosis

    On site

    We listen to what plays today in each zone and share what we find.

  3. Pilot in one zone

    1 month

    We start with the lobby, a waiting room or a hallway. We measure dwell time and feedback before taking it to the whole space.

  4. The whole space

    Monthly

    Once the pilot is approved, the identity plays in every zone and is refreshed every week.
